DotTube

YouTube for the CORSAIR XENEON EDGE

DotTube is an iCUE widget that brings YouTube to the XENEON EDGE touchscreen in a dot-matrix style. You sign in with your Google account, then browse your subscriptions, search and watch videos right on the Edge.

How it works

The widget runs inside iCUE on your own PC. It talks directly to Google's sign-in service and the YouTube Data API, using an OAuth client that you create in your own Google Cloud project. DotTube has no server of its own and does not collect anything.

This site also hosts the DotTube player relay, a single page that lets the embedded YouTube player run inside iCUE. It forwards player messages between the widget and YouTube and stores nothing.
