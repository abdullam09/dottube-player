Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 (browser handoff added)

This policy explains what the DotTube widget ("DotTube", "the widget") does with your information. In short: DotTube keeps everything on your own PC, has no server of its own and never sells or shares your data.

Google sign-in and the data DotTube accesses

DotTube uses YouTube API Services. When you sign in with Google, you grant DotTube the youtube scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). DotTube uses that access only to show you your YouTube content and to perform actions you ask for on the screen:

  • Read your channel name and picture, your subscriptions, your playlists and your liked videos.
  • Read public video, channel, comment and search information.
  • Only when you tap the matching button: like or unlike a video, subscribe to or unsubscribe from a channel, or add a video to one of your playlists.

DotTube never takes any of these actions on its own, and it does not upload videos, post comments, or delete videos or playlists.

Where your data is stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the widget's local storage on your PC, inside iCUE's profile for the widget. They are sent only to Google.
  • Watch history, resume points, Watch Later, the play queue and recent searches are kept only in that same local storage. They are not sent to YouTube or anywhere else.
  • Cached API responses (video titles, thumbnails, your subscription list) are kept locally for up to 24 hours to save quota, and then refreshed or discarded.
  • Your Google Client ID and Secret are stored in iCUE's widget settings on your PC.

The developer of DotTube does not receive, collect or have access to any of this data.

Who DotTube talks to

  • Google and YouTube (googleapis.com, youtube.com, youtube-nocookie.com and their image hosts), to sign you in, load content and play videos. Their use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • The player relay on this site, if you set it up. The widget loads it with the ID of the video you are playing. The relay forwards player messages and stores nothing. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • ntfy.sh, only if you use the optional DotTube Handoff browser extension. When you send a video from your browser, the extension posts the video ID, the current time and the video title to ntfy.sh on a channel named by your secret pairing code, and the widget receives it from there. Messages are sent with caching turned off, so ntfy.sh does not store them. ntfy.sh's handling of requests is covered by its privacy policy.

DotTube uses no analytics, advertising or tracking of its own. Videos play in YouTube's embedded player, which may show ads and use cookies under Google's policies.

Sharing

DotTube does not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

DotTube's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data and access

  • Sign out on the widget's Account page. This revokes DotTube's access with Google and deletes the tokens from your PC.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through the Google security settings page.
  • History and recent searches can be cleared in the widget, and Watch Later and queue items can be removed there.

Children

DotTube is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly handle their data.
